Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Summary

GTA Clipper runs on your own computer. It has no servers and no user database. We don't sell, share or analyse your data.

What the app accesses

  • TikTok: with your permission, your basic profile info (open ID, display name, avatar) to show which account is connected, and the video.upload permission to send your clips to your TikTok inbox as drafts.
  • Other platforms you connect (YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn): your account or page name and the permission to upload the clips you choose.
  • Videos you clip: processed on your computer. Only the finished clips you choose to post are uploaded, and only to the platform you choose.

Where it's stored

Sign-in tokens, settings and clips are stored only in files on your computer, in the app's folder. They are sent only to the platform they belong to (for example, a TikTok token is sent only to TikTok) when uploading or refreshing access.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with third parties. Videos and captions are sent to the platforms you post to, which handle them under their own privacy policies (for example the TikTok Privacy Policy). Transcripts may be sent to the AI service the app uses to pick moments and write titles.

Deleting your data

  • Click Disconnect in the app to delete a platform's stored sign-in.
  • Revoke access any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.
  • Delete the app's folder to remove everything else.

Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 13.
