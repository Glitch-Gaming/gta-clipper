GTA Clipper

A desktop tool that turns long gaming streams and videos into short vertical clips with commentary, and shares them to the creator's own TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn accounts.

Terms of Service Privacy Policy

What it does

  • Finds the strongest moments in a long video and cuts them into 9:16 clips with captions, on-screen commentary and a credit to the original creator.
  • Writes a title, caption and hashtags for each clip.
  • Uploads finished clips to the accounts the user connects. On TikTok, clips are sent to the user's TikTok inbox as drafts; the user reviews, edits and posts them in the TikTok app.

How TikTok is used

  • Login Kit: the user signs in with TikTok to connect their own account. We read only basic profile info (display name and avatar) to show which account is connected.
  • Content Posting API (video.upload): the user's own finished clips are uploaded to their TikTok inbox as drafts. Nothing is posted without the user finishing the post in TikTok.

Who it's for

GTA Clipper is used by its owner to publish their own edited clips. It runs on the user's computer; there is no public sign-up.
